An entry for [make.fun](https://make.fun)'s
`u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a` bounty: a
single sentence pitching a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny
everyday annoyance. One sentence, at most 220 characters.

## The pitch

> The Sock Reunion Service: a tiny drone that finds your missing sock, flies it home, and files a formal apology with the dryer for the separation.

The pitch is 145 characters, one sentence. The annoyance is real
and universal — a sock vanishes in the laundry — and the fix is
absurd in two directions at once: the rescue is far too grand (a
drone search-and-rescue mission for one sock) and the aftercare is
far too formal (a written apology, filed with the dryer, for the
separation). The dryer is treated as the guilty party and the sock
as a wronged citizen, which is the kind of misplaced seriousness
that makes an absurd invention feel like a real product.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) prints the pitch and
writes it to `/output/entry.txt`, after asserting it is still one
sentence of at most 220 characters:

```bash
python3 main.py
```

Output, verified locally:

```text
The Sock Reunion Service: a tiny drone that finds your missing sock, flies it home, and files a formal apology with the dryer for the separation.
```

145 characters, 1 sentence, 1 formal apology. The pitch was drafted
against the judge's order — funny invention first, clever fix second,
original idea third — and the fix is genuinely a fix: the sock does
come home, which is more than most lost socks ever get.
